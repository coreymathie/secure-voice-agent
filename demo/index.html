Cypress Harbor Credit Union

A fictional member-owned credit union used to show the agent at business scale: 92,400 members, $1.4B in assets, 11 branches in South Florida, 38 member-services agents.

Its members, calls and numbers are generated for this demo, and dates are shown relative to today. Safety test results are measured on the real code. Calls you place yourself are marked as test calls.

Generated by scripts/generate_sample_company.py and scripts/sample_calls.py from a fixed seed.
